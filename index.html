Alchemy

Most tools take as much of your attention as they can get. This one takes what the work needs, then stops. Nothing here leaves your browser.

  1. Inhale a thought, a link, a file. Seven at most.
  2. Settle for thirty seconds. Nothing is processed on arrival.
  3. Pulse: one word for what is loudest in the body.
  4. Reflect: what does this actually mean to you?
  5. Alchemize. Base matter becomes gold.
  6. Map where it connects, then keep it or release it.

Unattended items dissolve in 72 hours. Kept gold composts in 90 days. The slowness is the point; your liver doesn't rush either.

Alchemy is the practice companion for the PureLand method. Its loop rehearses the noticing that Attend, the method's second step, asks for. A tool with deliberate constraints, not a validated instrument.

Keys: 1–4 switch views, n captures, esc goes back.
